## 🌟 Highlights of the Blueprint
- **🎨 Figma-Caliber Design System:** Modern glassmorphism, curated luxury color palettes, typography hierarchy (`Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Inter`), and 60fps micro-animations.
- **🔒 Bank-Grade Security:** OWASP Top 10 mitigation, token-based authentication (JWT + HttpOnly cookies), MFA/TOTP, and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
- **🎠 Interactive Showcase Slides:** Responsive, touch-enabled hero slider with kinetic typography and smooth transitions.
- **📊 Executive & Customer Dashboards:** Comprehensive KPI metrics, revenue charts, order lifecycle management, and inventory controls.
- **🧪 Testing & Quality Assurance:** Test pyramid strategy covering unit tests, integration tests, and Playwright end-to-end user journeys.
- **🤖 Zero-Confusion AI Architecture:** Standardized folder structure and clear guidelines designed specifically to prevent AI ambiguity or hallucinations.
